Built from the ECO proposal

The proposal and diagram define five connected modules. This platform turns them into a mobile-friendly web experience using the Cricket SA brand colours and familiar account-to-dashboard navigation.

1

Player Production

Profiles, scouting notes, performance tracking, pathway status and squad shortlists.

 2

Database Management

Central records, document storage, role permissions, data validation and backups.

 3

Reports Basket

Board dashboards, saved reports, exports and scheduled reporting packs.

 4

Fixtures

League setup, automated fixture generation, scoring, results and notifications.

 5

Empowerment

Training, assessments, certificates, safeguarding, anti-corruption and first aid tracking.

One federation database, many role-based views

Administrators, board members, selectors, club officials, coaches, umpires, scorers, players, parents and the public can each see the right level of information.

Governance

Audit trails, controlled access and reporting give leadership cleaner oversight of records and decisions.

Safe cricket

Safeguarding, first aid, integrity training and certificate renewals sit inside the same operating system.

Low-bandwidth ready

The platform is shaped for mobile access, offline-friendly capture and areas where connectivity is limited.

Implementation roadmap

The proposal recommends staged delivery when the board prefers a controlled rollout.

1. DiscoveryStakeholder workshops, data audit and signed-off specification.
2. DesignWireframes, branding, user journeys and database design.
3. BuildDevelopment of all five modules across website and mobile-ready views.
4. TestingUser testing, security testing and migration of existing records.
5. LaunchTraining, pilot clubs, public launch and first-season helpdesk.
